An offline, AI-driven Bitcoin transaction intelligence platform that ingests bulk blockchain metadata (TXID, wallet addresses, amounts) alongside network-layer metadata (source/destination IP, port, timing) and fuses both layers into a single investigative graph. Unsupervised anomaly detection (Isolation Forest) and entity clustering (HDBSCAN) run on engineered behavioral and graph features to surface a ranked list of potentially suspicious wallets and transactions — each alert backed by a transparent, decomposable risk score and a plain-language explanation, not a black-box output.

  • Bitcoin's pseudonymity is broken down by correlating on-chain wallet behavior with off-chain IP/timing observations — the core NTRO ask.
  • Replaces manual, rule-based triage with a working trained ML model, satisfying the PS's explicit "not just rules" requirement.
  • Delivers investigation-ready output (ranked alerts, evidence, confidence scores) instead of raw scores an analyst has to reinterpret.
  • Dual-layer correlation (blockchain + network) rather than chain-analysis alone, which is what most commercial tools offer.
  • Every alert is explainable by design — top contributing features, graph evidence, and cluster context are shown together, not just a score.
  • Fully offline, self-contained architecture — no cloud dependency, deployable in air-gapped investigative environments.

04

Feasibility and Viability

  • Organizers provide a synthetic dataset modeled on real Bitcoin P2P/transaction fields — no dependency on live or seized data to build and validate the prototype.
  • Every chosen algorithm (Isolation Forest, HDBSCAN, SHAP) is mature, well-documented, and runs fully offline on standard hardware — no GPU or cloud dependency.
  • Architecture is modular: ingestion, graph, ML, and dashboard can be built and tested independently before integration.
RiskMitigation
No ground-truth labels in the synthetic dataset Inject scripted criminal-pattern scenarios (layering, mixer-like fan-in/out) as pseudo ground-truth for evaluation
Risk-score weights could look arbitrary to judges Document weighting rationale explicitly and calibrate against injected scenarios rather than presenting fixed numbers as final
Graph can become large/cluttered Subgraph filtering by risk threshold; render only the neighborhood around a selected alert

Impact and Benefits

  • National Technical Research Organisation and allied cybercrime investigation units get a shortlist of high-priority entities instead of manually reviewing raw blockchain dumps.
  • Reduces time-to-lead for investigators tracing ransomware, darknet-market, and extortion payment flows.
  • Gives non-blockchain-expert analysts an accessible, explainable entry point into complex chain analysis.
  • Security: strengthens India's cyber-financial crime investigation capability against ransomware, laundering, and extortion.
  • Operational: cuts manual investigative effort by pre-ranking entities by risk, with evidence attached.
  • Sovereign capability: fully offline, indigenously built — no dependency on foreign commercial blockchain-analytics vendors for sensitive investigations.
